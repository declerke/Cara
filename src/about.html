About Cara

A familiar storefront identity, presented as a local browser demo.

A student storefront, refreshed

A small shop interface with a clear demo boundary

Cara began as a university storefront assignment. This rebuild keeps its name, clothing catalog, KSh sample prices, and supplied imagery while making the pages and shopping path work together.

The catalog and cart are examples for browsing in this browser. There is no connected store, customer service team, account system, fulfilment, or payment service behind the page.
